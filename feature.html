Features of Phytomax

Explore the innovative features that make Phytomax the ultimate plant monitoring solution.

Real-Time Monitoring

Track soil moisture, temperature, and humidity levels in real-time with DHT11 and soil moisture sensors.

Remote Control

Use the mobile application to irrigate your plants remotely, ensuring they get the care they need anytime.

Plant Disease Detection

Leverage advanced computer vision with TensorFlow Lite to detect plant diseases and provide timely interventions.

Custom Crop Recommendations

Get personalized crop suggestions based on soil type, moisture levels, and temperature data.

IoT Integration

Connect seamlessly with IoT devices like NodeMCU ESP8266 to transfer data to Arduino Cloud for advanced analytics.

Mobile App

Monitor your plants' health, get alerts, and access real-time data through our user-friendly mobile app.

Environmental Insights

Analyze environmental conditions to make informed decisions about plant care and optimize growth.

Photo Recognition

Identify plant species and diagnose health issues using our integrated photo recognition system.

Data Visualization

View comprehensive data visualizations to understand trends and optimize your plant care routines.
